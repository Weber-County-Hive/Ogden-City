Ogden's project areas

Where Ogden's Redevelopment Agency uses tax increment, when each area ends, and how much property value has grown in each, from the RDA's own January 2025 annual update.

WHAT THIS IS
Ogden Redevelopment Agency project areas
  • Source: RDA Annual Update, January 2025 (the RDA's FY24 annual report).
  • Active: 11 project areas listed, expiring 2024 to 2058.
  • Closed: 16 older project areas, 1979–2022.

In plain words

A project area is a piece of the city where, for a set number of years, the agreed share of the growth in property taxes is allocated to the RDA to spend on projects there. Owners still pay their full tax bill, and the county treasurer collects it. When an area expires, the growth is then distributed to the city, school district, county and other taxing entities. This page lists Ogden's areas so you can see where one is near you and when it ends.

Explain it like I'm 10

  • A project area is a part of the city picked for building projects.
  • Inside it, property values usually go up over time, so property taxes grow.
  • Owners still pay their full tax bill, and the county treasurer collects it.
  • For a set number of years, the agreed share of that growth goes to the RDA to spend on projects there.
  • When the area ends, all of it is distributed to the city, schools, county and others.

The short version

  • The RDA's January 2025 update lists 11 active project areas with a combined value increase of $639,423,617.
  • The latest-ending: Continental (WonderBlock) runs to 2058 (its listing shows 2047/2058); Trackline and Airport run to 2047.
  • Fairmount and Lincoln Avenue were listed to expire in 2024.
  • Newer areas not on the January 2025 list are tracked on their own pages: Wildcat Square (PIDS-Utah) and the Gibson Grove area (OC 001).

Newer areas and activity

  • Wildcat Square: student housing near Weber State; see the Hive's PIDS-Utah case file.
  • Gibson Grove (the Nov. 10, 2026 staff report calls it the "proposed Gibson CRA"): the RDA's land at and near 1781 Gibson Ave.; see OC 001.

What we don't know yet

  • Each area's tax increment received to date and its total budget cap (the annual update lists value increase, not dollars collected).
  • Which taxing entities participate in each area, and at what percentage.
  • Whether Fairmount and Lincoln Avenue closed on schedule in 2024.
  • Boundary maps for each area.

Every item above is also a question you can ask. See Questions to ask, and whom to ask.

Questions to ask, and whom to ask

  1. Ogden RDA: Will the RDA post, for each project area, the increment received each year, the budget cap and the participating entities?
  2. Ogden School District and Weber County: How much of your share of the growth goes to Ogden project areas each year?
  3. Ogden RDA: Did the Fairmount and Lincoln Avenue project areas close on schedule in 2024, and where did their remaining money go?
  4. Ogden RDA: Will boundary maps for every project area be posted online?
