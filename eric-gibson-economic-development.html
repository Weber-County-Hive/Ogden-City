RECORDS NOTE · OGDEN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT · OC 010

Eric Gibson, Ogden's economic development director

Who runs Ogden's Economic Development Division, where he worked before, and his role in the 1781 Gibson Ave. sale. A short records page.

Office: Economic Development DivisionStarted: Dec. 8, 2025Records: Ogden City documents

Posted Nov. 6, 2026

WHO AND WHAT
Eric Gibson · Ogden City Economic Development Division
  • Role: Ogden City Economic Development Director.
  • Start date: Dec. 8, 2025, according to the city's Community & Economic Development weekly brief.
  • Before Ogden: "A decade with Better City organization," according to the Ogden RDA's Feb. 10, 2026 minutes.
  • 1781 Gibson Ave.: The RDA's Jan. 22, 2026 request for proposals says "Prepared by Eric Gibson, Economic Development." He is the staff contact on the Nov. 10, 2026 staff report.

In plain words

Ogden's economic development director helps bring businesses and projects to the city and works on land deals like the 1781 Gibson Ave. sale. Eric Gibson started the job in December 2025. Before that he worked for about ten years with Better City, a consulting organization that works with cities on development. His name is on the request for proposals for the Gibson land and on the staff report the council will hear Nov. 10. He has the same last name as lobbyist Kerry Gibson, but no record we've seen shows they are related.

The short version

  • Dec. 8, 2025: starts as Ogden's Economic Development Division director (city weekly brief).
  • Before: "a decade with Better City organization" (Ogden RDA minutes, Feb. 10, 2026).
  • Jan. 22, 2026: the 1781 Gibson Ave. request for proposals is "Prepared by Eric Gibson, Economic Development."
  • Nov. 10, 2026: listed as the staff contact on the Gibson land sale staff report (OC 001).
  • Not related to lobbyist Kerry Gibson in any record we've seen. The city's bio says he was raised in Colorado Springs and has lived in Utah since 2014.

What the city's records say

Ogden Community & Economic Development weekly brief, Dec. 8, 2025"Welcome to our new Economic Development Division Director, Eric Gibson (photo right) – who starts today, Dec. 8"
Ogden RDA minutes, Feb. 10, 2026"the City's new Economic Development Director, Eric Gibson, … who joined the City in December after working for a decade with Better City organization."

The city's "Meet the Team" page says he has worked in economic development for more than 10 years supporting cities, counties and regional authorities, focusing on market, feasibility and impact analyses. He studied at Weber State University and holds a master's degree in economics from Purdue University. Utah Business named him to its 2026 Forty Under 40 list.

His role in the 1781 Gibson Ave. sale

  • The RDA's Jan. 22, 2026 request for proposals for infill housing at 1781 Gibson Ave. says "Prepared by Eric Gibson, Economic Development."
  • The Nov. 10, 2026 work session staff report, which proposes selling about 10.8 acres to Lync Development, LLC for $5.1 million, lists him as the staff contact.
  • The proposals were scored by a committee of city staff. The records we've read don't list the committee members.

Full deal: OC 001, 1781 Gibson Ave.

Better City

Better City is a consulting organization that works with cities on downtown and economic development. The RDA minutes say Gibson worked with it for about a decade before joining Ogden. Earlier Hive research (June 2026) identified him as Better City's senior director of strategic analysis; we haven't confirmed that title in a public record, so it isn't stated as fact here.

The Hive's June 2026 WonderBlock research looked at whether Better City advised Ogden on WonderBlock. We have not found a contract or council record showing that, so this page makes no such claim.

What we don't know yet

  • His exact title and projects at Better City, and whether any of Better City's work was for Ogden City or its RDA.
  • Who served on the staff committee that scored the four 1781 Gibson Ave. proposals.
  • Whether he has filed a conflict-of-interest disclosure with the city, as some employees are required to do.

Every item above is also a question you can ask. See Questions to ask, and whom to ask.

Questions to ask, and whom to ask

  1. Ogden City administration: Who was on the scoring committee for the 1781 Gibson Ave. proposals?
  2. Ogden City administration: Has Better City had any contract with Ogden City or the RDA, and when?
  3. Ogden City Council (public comment): When the city hired its economic development director, did it check whether any of his past Better City work involved Ogden City or the RDA?
  4. Ogden City Recorder: Which city employees file conflict-of-interest disclosures, and are they open to the public?
