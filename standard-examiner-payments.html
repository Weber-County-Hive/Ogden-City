Ogden City and the Standard-Examiner: the payments

State spending data shows what Ogden City has paid the company that publishes the Standard-Examiner each year since 2014. In fiscal 2026 the total more than doubled, to $131,472.

Payer: Ogden CityVendor: Ogden Publishing Corporation (Standard-Examiner)Source: Transparent Utah

Posted Nov. 5, 2026 · Updated Nov. 6, 2026

WHAT WAS CHECKED
Transparent Utah vendor payments, all governments, vendor names "Standard Examiner," "Standard-Examiner," "Ogden Publishing," "Ogden Standard," "Sandusky" and "Standard Media"
  • Paid by: Ogden City (and, for comparison, other governments)
  • Paid to: listed as "STANDARD EXAMINER" through FY2017 and "OGDEN PUBLISHING CORPORATION" from FY2018
  • Searched: Nov. 5, 2026. Raw, unaudited data as posted by each government.

In plain words

Cities pay local newspapers for things like legal notices, ads and subscriptions. Utah's state auditor posts what each government paid each vendor every year. Ogden City paid the Standard-Examiner's company $57,870 in fiscal 2025 and $131,472 in fiscal 2026. The state data shows the totals, not what the money bought.

Explain it like I'm 10

  • Cities buy things from newspapers, like ads and legal notices.
  • Utah's state auditor posts a list of what each city paid each company every year.
  • Ogden City paid the company that owns the Standard-Examiner $57,870 in 2025.
  • In 2026 it paid $131,472. That is more than twice as much.
  • The list shows the totals. It does not show what the money bought.

The short version

  • Ogden City paid the Standard-Examiner's publisher, Ogden Publishing Corporation, $131,472.38 in fiscal 2026, up from $57,869.85 in fiscal 2025.
  • From fiscal 2022 through the partial 2027 year, Ogden City paid $335,199.11, more than any other government in the state data: about twice Davis School District and more than four times Weber County.
  • Weber County's payments fell from $18,012.54 (FY2022) to $7,333.67 (FY2026).
  • The data doesn't show what the payments were for or why 2026 jumped.

Cities commonly pay newspapers for legal notices, ads and subscriptions. Which of these Ogden paid for is not in the state data.

The question for readers

A news outlet that covers a city can also be paid by that city. The record shows Ogden City is the largest government payer of the Standard-Examiner's company in the state data since 2022, and that its payments more than doubled in fiscal 2026. The record does not show what the money bought or any effect on coverage. Readers can weigh for themselves how a newsroom's coverage of a city fits with that city being its largest government customer, and what disclosure would help them judge.

What we don't know yet

  • What the payments were for: legal notices, advertising, subscriptions or something else.
  • Why fiscal 2026 more than doubled.
  • How much of it, if any, was for public notices the city is required by law to publish.
  • Whether there is a contract or agreement behind the payments, and who approved it.

Every item above is also a question you can ask. See Questions to ask, and whom to ask.

Questions to ask, and whom to ask

  • Ogden City Recorder: How much of the payments were for public notices the city is required by law to publish, and how much was for other things?
  • Ogden City Management Services (finance): What did the FY2026 payments to Ogden Publishing Corporation cover, by item?
  • Ogden City Council: Is there a contract or council-approved agreement with the Standard-Examiner's publisher?
  • Readers, when reading local coverage: Does the paper disclose payments from governments it covers?
  • Ogden City Management Services (finance): Why did payments to Ogden Publishing Corporation rise from $57,870 in FY2025 to $131,472 in FY2026?
  • Ogden City Recorder: Is the agreement with Ogden Publishing Corporation on file, and when and by whom was it approved?
